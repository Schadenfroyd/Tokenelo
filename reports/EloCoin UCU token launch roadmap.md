# Verify the compute before minting EloCoin

The fastest credible path gets a guarded EloCoin mainnet and Base DEX launch out around **mid-April 2027, about 26 weeks from now**. Coinbase and Kraken listings would realistically follow in **May–October 2027**. Getting there takes three moves Tokenelo has not made yet: set up a **separate token entity**, publish a **versioned UCU-v1 specification**, and anchor every mint to **usage the provider has verified** instead of Tokenelo's editable local logs. Today the repo holds documentation only. It has no source code, license, tests or UCU formula, and it states publicly that UCU "does not establish ... token issuance, redemption, or a financial return" ([README](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/README.md#L37)). The precedents are harsh. Compute networks grew real revenue while their tokens collapsed, and tokens paid out for activity were farmed into the ground. So EloCoin has to pay for verified work out of a **capped emission pool**, at a reward rate below the cost of faking that work. US law offers no statute and no final safe harbor: the CLARITY Act failed cloture **49–50 on Sept 15, 2026** ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/senate-cloture-vote-on-clarity-act-fails-dealing-regulatory-setback-to-crypto-industry.html)). The SEC's March 2026 interpretation also excludes task-based "earn by doing" distributions from its airdrop safe conclusion ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/03/sec-and-cftc-issue-interpretive-release-on-crypto)). The design therefore has to look like DePIN-style programmatic payment for verified contribution, with **no US public sale and no USD redemption**. Budget roughly **$0.5M cash for a lean launch, or $2–3M+ to be ready for tier-1 exchanges**, plus 5–20% of supply. Trading compute as a commodity is a separate, later track. Regulated compute futures settle against GPU-hour price indices, so the commodity opportunity is a **UCU index and credits redeemable for compute**, not the floating token. This is a research synthesis, not legal advice. Every regulatory step needs US and EU counsel.

## Tokenelo is a docs-only tracker that publicly disclaims a token

The repo has three commits, all made on Oct 7, 2026. The product is in "internal alpha" with no public source, binary or license ([README](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/README.md#L9)). Apache-2.0 is only an "approved licensing plan", and the licensor entity has not been named ([licensing.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/docs/licensing.md#L5)). All **seven public-alpha roadmap items are unchecked** ([roadmap.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/docs/roadmap.md#L7-L13)). UCU has **no public formula, no expansion of the acronym, no calibration data and no earning mechanism**. The tracker promises "no ... crypto wallet, or reward mechanism" ([README](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/README.md#L25)), and the only mention of EloCoin says it "is a separate product pursuit and is not part of the tracker launch" ([licensing.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/docs/licensing.md#L7)). **Do not rewrite those disclaimers.** Reversing them days after publishing would damage the credibility the tracker sells to procurement and FinOps buyers. The README already makes room for future hosted services "with separate terms". EloCoin should be that: an opt-in service run by a separate entity that consumes verified usage data.

The deeper gap is trust. Tokenelo parses local tool logs into a SQLite ledger the user can write to ([privacy.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/docs/privacy.md#L7)). Its threat model explicitly excludes the local administrator ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/SECURITY.md#L17)), and invoice reconciliation is only "proposed" ([data-accuracy.md](https://github.com/Schadenfroyd/Tokenelo/blob/30a5cea/docs/data-accuracy.md#L37)). Once usage mints money, the user becomes the adversary. Edited database rows, fabricated logs, a rogue metering proxy, self-metered local inference and sybil installs would each mint free coins. **Tokenelo's role is therefore to supply the measurement methodology and the user experience, not to be the trust root.** Its reusable assets are real: per-component token fields, rules that stop subsets being counted twice, distinct unknown and estimated states, rate provenance and a versioned price table. Together these are the skeleton of a UCU-v1 spec. Before any token work, the founder also has to:

- settle the legal entity and licensor;
- issue the license and publish the source, because Coinbase's listing questionnaire asks for source-code links and audits ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/A-Guide-to-the-Digital-Asset-Listing-Process-at-Coinbase));
- rewrite the threat model to treat the user as an adversary;
- add a remediation SLA and a bug bounty;
- write a data inventory and privacy terms for usage uploads.

## Compute networks won on usage while their tokens lost value

Decentralized compute is real, but its tokens have not held value. The DePIN sector earns about **$82M a year in on-chain revenue against a market cap of about $7B** ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/view/depin/)). Render saw an **86% price decline alongside rising activity** ([CoinAlertNews](https://coinalertnews.com/news/2026/05/08/render-network-activity-2026)). Every major network has since moved to dollar pricing with a token sink. Akash's burn-and-mint system went live on **March 23, 2026**: buyers burn AKT to mint ACT, a non-transferable, USD-denominated credit ([TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/20776)). io.net replaced fixed emissions with USD-denominated payouts and burns funded by revenue in June 2026 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/research/the-incentive-dynamic-engine-a-new-era-for-io-net-tokenomics)). Helium's Data Credits are fixed at $0.00001 and cannot be transferred ([Gemini](https://www.gemini.com/cryptopedia/helium-network-token-map-helium-hotspot-hnt-coin)). **No live token is pegged to, or redeemable for, a fixed quantity of compute.** Every stable layer pegs to USD, and the volatile token is burned or minted around it.

Use-to-earn tokens fail in a predictable way. STEPN's GST **fell 98% in two months** once new users stopped arriving ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/is-move-to-earn-dead-stepns-gst-token-tumbles-98-in-2-months/)). Grass airdropped 10% of supply to about 2.8M wallets with no lockup, ended up **about 90% below its all-time high** ([OwnYourMind](https://ownyourmind.ai/projects/grass/)), and paid Season 2 in **USDC from revenue** rather than in new tokens ([airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/grass/)). Across 2025, **84.7% of 118 token launches traded below their launch valuation, with a median drop of 71%** ([MEXC News](https://www.mexc.com/news/338692)). The exception was Hyperliquid, which combined real revenue, no VC allocation and a usage-based airdrop of about 31% of supply ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/how-hyperliquid-airdrop-changed-game)). For EloCoin, real usage alone will not hold up the price. Rewards must cost the network less than the work they buy, and the token needs a sink.

Compute became an exchange-traded commodity in 2026, but not in the form EloCoin imagines. CME scheduled cash-settled **H100 and B200 rental-index futures, each covering 730 GPU-hours, for Oct 5, 2026**, using Silicon Data indices ([CME](https://www.cmegroup.com/media-room/press-releases/2026/8/11/cme_group_and_silicondatatolaunchcomputefuturesonoctober5tounloc.html)). Whether trading actually began is unconfirmed. ICE's Ornn contract had no listing date as of Sept 29 ([ICE](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/futures_us/exchange_notices/ICE_Futures_US_Exchange_Notice_ORNN_Compute_4Q2026_20260929.pdf)), and Kalshi published GPU forward curves on July 14 ([Markets Media](https://www.marketsmedia.com/kalshi-offers-compute-forward-curves/)). Two patterns hold across these markets. Every contract is quoted in **$/GPU-hour for a specific chip**, and the commercial winners are **index providers**, not token issuers. Defining UCU by LLM tokens would be a broken unit: constant-capability inference prices fall roughly 10x a year ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/llm-inference-price-trends)), while frontier-model token prices rose **101% year on year** ([BenchLM](https://benchlm.ai/stats/llm-pricing)). EloCoin's defensible differentiator is a **hardware-normalized UCU**, analogous to Render's benchmark-weighted OctaneBench-hour ([Render](https://rendernetwork.com/pricing)), published as an index that can be hedged against CME.

## US law favors pay-for-verified-work over usage rebates

The governing framework is the SEC–CFTC interpretation of **March 17, 2026**. It sorts tokens into five categories, and a "digital commodity" must work without a **central party that distributes rewards** ([Paul Weiss](https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-memos/sec-and-cftc-release-interpretation-on-application-of-federal-securities-laws-to-crypto-assets)). An EloCoin whose attestor decides each user's reward probably does not qualify at launch. The interpretation's safe conclusion for airdrops covers only distributions given for nothing, and it **excludes rewards for tasks, purchases or "providing value"**. Mining and staking rewards, by contrast, count as payment for services ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/03/sec-and-cftc-issue-interpretive-release-on-crypto)). The closest favorable precedents are the DoubleZero and Fuse no-action letters for programmatic DePIN rewards. Both apply only to their own facts ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-09-30-sec-grants-doublezero-no-action-relief-over-token-distribution-372881); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sec-no-action-letter-fuse-depin-project)).

EloCoin should therefore pay for **verified contribution of usage data to the UCU network and index**. The formula should be deterministic, published and enforced on-chain, with no discretionary adjustments, no yield and no price promises. The interpretation adds a test of whether the issuer made "affirmative promises" of managerial effort ([Lowenstein](https://www.lowenstein.com/news-insights/publications/client-alerts/sec-issues-interpretive-framework-for-crypto-asset-classification-fctm)), which makes **disciplined marketing a compliance control** in its own right. That rules out promising buybacks, listings or price appreciation.

One tension needs to be settled first. Rewards proportional to spend are the best defense against gaming, but counsel may read them as a "purchase plus rebate". A mitigating fact is that the money goes to Anthropic or OpenAI, not to the issuer, and nothing is bought from Tokenelo. No source has analyzed this question, so it is **counsel question #1**, and a DoubleZero-style no-action request is the optional backstop.

There is no safe harbor to wait for. The SEC proposed Regulation Crypto Assets on Aug 21, 2026, with **comments due Oct 20, 2026** ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/21/2026-17183/regulation-crypto-assets)). Its startup exemption allows up to **$5M over four years with no lockup** ([Journal of Accountancy](https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2026/aug/sec-proposal-aims-to-clarify-securities-rules-for-crypto-assets/)), and it treats network rewards as "covered transactions" ([Troutman](https://www.troutman.com/insights/key-takeaways-from-the-secs-proposed-regulation-crypto-assets/)). Final rules are not expected **before Q1 2027** ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/sec-proposes-regulation-crypto-assets-rulemaking)). Until then, raise money only through Reg D 506(c) and Reg S SAFTs with lockups, and **sell nothing publicly to US persons**.

The other US rules shape what the token can promise:

- **Money transmission.** FinCEN treats an issuer that can both issue and redeem a token as a money transmitter ([FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20CVC%20Guidance%20FINAL.pdf)). Run no issuer fiat-redemption window, and leave cash-out to exchanges.
- **Stablecoin law.** Redeeming in dollars would make EloCoin a GENIUS Act payment stablecoin, with rules enforced by Jan 18, 2027 ([King & Spalding](https://www.kslaw.com/news-and-insights/stablecoin-issuers-as-banks-fincen-and-ofac-issue-comprehensive-aml-and-sanctions-rules-under-the-genius-act)). **Never peg the token to USD.**
- **State licenses.** California's DFAL licensing went live July 1, 2026, and sources conflict on whether it covers issuing redeemable assets ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/alerts-finance-dcb-california-dfal-license-application-open); [MoFo](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260729-california-digital-assets-and-fintech-update)). The NY BitLicense reportedly covers issuing virtual currency ([InnReg](https://www.innreg.com/blog/bitlicense-new-york)). **Geofence NY, and probably CA, at the claim step** until counsel clears them.
- **Commodities law.** The CFTC polices fraud and manipulation in spot trading, and its Oct 5, 2026 proposal would route leveraged retail crypto through registered futures brokers (FCMs) ([Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/cftc-proposes-first-crypto-market-rules/)). EloCoin must not offer synthetic or leveraged exposure to compute prices.
- **Tax.** Rewards are ordinary income at fair market value on receipt ([Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/learn/staking-taxes)). That means W-9/W-8 collection above reporting thresholds, and a self-custodial claim flow so Tokenelo does not become a broker.

The EU is the secondary market. EloCoin would be an "other crypto-asset" under MiCA, and its white paper must be **notified to the home regulator 20 working days before publication**, without prior approval ([AMF](https://www.amf-france.org/en/professionals/fintech/my-relations-amf/public-offerings-and-admission-trading-crypto-assets)). Since **July 1, 2026**, only MiCA-authorized crypto-asset service providers may serve EU clients ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/ESMA75-113276571-1710_Public_Statement_MiCA_transitional_period_ends.pdf)), and Kraken requires a notified white paper for any listing in the EEA ([Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/gb/get-listed)). MiCA's reward and free-offer exemptions do not cover admission to trading ([MiCA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj)). The fastest option is to geo-block the EU at launch and add it after notification.

The recommended structure is the standard one: **Elo AI as the US development company, plus a Cayman foundation that owns a BVI token issuer** ([Legal Nodes](https://www.legalnodes.com/article/cayman-foundation-bvi-company-token-launches)). Distribute tokens by smart contract, because Cayman's VASP Act can apply when tokens are issued for consideration ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=90e058b4-bff3-46c7-8111-86c3be3e701b)). Keep insider and treasury holdings below 20% so a revived CLARITY Act's maturity test stays within reach.

## Mint from provider APIs, cap the pool and price rewards below the cost of faking

**Verification sets the scope of the launch.** The only production-grade anchor for Claude and OpenAI usage is the providers' own admin usage APIs. Anthropic's Usage & Cost API reports usage by key, workspace and model, but it is **"unavailable for individual accounts"** ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/usage-cost-api)). Anthropic also offers Claude Code analytics per user per day ([Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/claude-code-analytics-api)), and OpenAI exposes comparable organization-level usage and cost endpoints ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/completions_usage_api)). Subscription usage on Claude Pro/Max and ChatGPT plans is not exposed. **EloCoin's launch audience is therefore API-billed organizations, which happen to be Tokenelo's existing FinOps and procurement buyers.**

| Tier | Evidence | Minting policy |
|---|---|---|
| A | Provider admin usage and cost APIs | Mint, with deduplication on provider org and key IDs |
| B | Hardware-attested (TEE) self-hosted inference | Later, discounted: the TEE.fail attack extracts attestation keys with under $1,000 of equipment and physical access ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2025/10/new-teefail-side-channel-attack.html)) |
| C | Tokenelo local logs | Display only, never mint |

Zero-knowledge proofs of model inference reach only GPT-2 and Gemma-3 scale ([Lagrange](https://lagrange.dev/blog/deepprove-is-now-open-source)). Chainlink Functions was sunset on Sept 1, 2026 ([Chainlink](https://functions.chain.link/mainnet)), so any later move to a decentralized attestor should target its replacement, Chainlink's CRE.

The minimum viable launch has four parts. An **attestor service** pulls Tier A usage every day, converts it to UCU-v1, runs anomaly checks, and posts one Merkle root per epoch, signed with a key held in a key-management service. On Base, three contracts carry the token:

- **EloToken**: a capped, non-upgradeable OpenZeppelin v5 ERC-20 that inherits `ERC20Bridgeable` (the ERC-7802 standard), so a bridge can be added later without migrating the token ([OpenZeppelin](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/api/token/erc20)).
- **EpochMinter**: enforces epoch caps, a 3–7 day challenge window, a guardian veto, and 30–90 day vesting with clawback.
- **UCURegistry**: holds versioned conversion factors behind a timelock.

Governance runs from a 3-of-5 Safe multisig through a 48–72 hour timelock, with a separate pause guardian. **Stay on one chain at launch.** The **$292M** KelpDAO loss came from a bridge set up with a single verifier ([Blockaid](https://www.blockaid.io/blog/how-a-single-layerzero-dvn-compromise-drained-292m-from-kelpdao)). Budget **$20–60K for the audit, $5–20K for a re-audit, and 3–6 weeks**, and book the auditor early ([Zealynx](https://www.zealynx.io/blogs/audit-pricing-2026); [Sherlock](https://www.sherlock.xyz/post/smart-contract-audit-pricing-a-market-reference-for-2026)). A team of 2–3 engineers can reach a guarded mainnet in about 4–6 months. That estimate is the research team's inference, not a benchmark.

The tokenomics follow one rule: **the reward for a UCU must be worth less than the cheapest way to produce that UCU.** Set the token value per $1 of verified spend at about 1–5%. Exclude flat-rate subscription usage and any source without a provider anchor. Require KYB above set thresholds, and use Human Passport scoring for small retail claims ([Human Passport](https://passport.human.tech/blog/human-passport-proof-of-personhood-and-sybil-resistance-for-web3)). The supply design has four parts:

- a hard cap, for example 1B tokens;
- **30–50% of supply in a usage-mining pool** released on a declining schedule, split pro rata by verified UCU in each epoch, with per-account caps, and never a 1 UCU = 1 ELO peg;
- **at least 15–20% float at launch**;
- insider cliffs of at least 12 months, and seasonal airdrops totalling 5–15%.

Define UCU-v1 in **H100-equivalent GPU-seconds**. That unit deflates far more slowly than per-token prices, and it can be priced against the Silicon Data H100 index that CME references ([Silicon Data](https://www.silicondata.com/products/silicon-index/h100)). This is where EloCoin and compute commodity trading meet. Add an Akash-style sink only once partner providers can actually deliver compute: users burn ELO for **non-transferable UCU credits**, with a time-weighted oracle price and circuit breakers ([Akash](https://akash.network/blog/what-burn-mint-equilibrium-means-for-akash/)). Use that programmatic burn instead of discretionary buybacks, which run into the marketing-promise problem.

## Launch on a Base DEX first and win Coinbase and Kraken with traction

Launch through either a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction or a curated sale, then open a **single official Base pool with locked liquidity**. The auction format let Aztec raise about $59–61M from more than 16,700 bidders, with per-wallet caps ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/381618/aztec-network-raises-over-60-million-in-eth-with-community-first-token-sale-testing-new-auction-model)). Coinbase's token-sale platform runs about **one sale a month** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/378177/coinbase-public-token-sales-ico-platform-monad-first)). Avoid pump.fun-style bonding curves. Their graduation rates are tiny and they signal "memecoin" ([The Block](https://theblock.co/post/409815/pump-fun-token-graduation-rate-jumps-boost-changes-launch-incentives)).

Base gives three access routes:

- **Coinbase DEX trading** reaches US users outside NY with no listing application ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/373940/coinbase-integrates-dex-trading-within-its-exchange-for-us-users-on-base-network)).
- **CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap** list a token once it trades on a venue they track ([CoinGecko](https://support.coingecko.com/hc/en-us/articles/7291312302617)). Submit within 72 hours of launch.
- **Central listings are free but gated.** Coinbase reviews legal, compliance and security and takes "hours to months" ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/A-Guide-to-the-Digital-Asset-Listing-Process-at-Coinbase)). Kraken acknowledges applications in about **2 weeks**, then needs **2–6 weeks to integrate** ([Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/gb/get-listed)).

Gensyn listed on Coinbase and Kraken at launch in April 2026 and still dropped about 45% within hours ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/price-prediction/gensyn-ai-listed-45percent-crashed-in-hours)), so listing alone does not support the price. Skip the tier-2 offshore exchanges. Their listing fees are unverified estimates of **$50–800K**, and they block US users ([Tokpie](https://tokpie.io/blog/crypto-exchange-listing-fees-2026/)).

Hire a market maker on a **retainer plus an inventory loan**, with spread, depth and uptime targets written in for each venue, and never pay for volume. GSR's public proposal to Stake DAO was $100K setup, $20K a month and a $1M loan ([Spark](https://www.spark.money/tools/crypto-market-maker-comparison)). Since March 2026, Binance requires projects to disclose their market makers and bans profit-sharing arrangements ([e27](https://e27.co/binance-cracks-down-on-market-makers-what-traders-need-to-know-now-20260326/)). Movement's market-maker dump ended in a co-founder's exit ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/317524/movement-labs-suspends-co-founder-amid-market-maker-controversy)), and the DOJ has prosecuted market makers for wash trading ([DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ma/pr/eighteen-individuals-and-entities-charged-international-operation-targeting-widespread)). **Disclose the market maker and loan terms at launch.**

- file the Reg Crypto Assets comment and book an auditor this month;

Going faster is not credible. A token with no verified minting would either have to be sold, which is a securities offering, or be farmed, which repeats STEPN.

The risks rank in this order:

1. **Provider dependence.** Anthropic or OpenAI could forbid or cut off use of admin-API data for rewards. Nobody has checked their terms on this, and it is the token's single point of failure.
2. **Securities classification.** A usage rebate could be read as a task-based reward or a purchase that triggers Howey, and private plaintiffs are not bound by the SEC's safe harbor.
3. **Gaming.** Wash usage and sybil farming, if the reward rate drifts above cost.
4. **Price collapse after launch,** which is now the base rate.
5. **Liability for the market maker's conduct.**
6. **Damage to Tokenelo's brand** with procurement buyers.

## Conclusion

The research reframes what EloCoin is. The durable asset is a **trusted, hardware-normalized UCU measurement with verified data behind it**. The token is only the mechanism that pays organizations to contribute that data. The institutional compute market is already forming around index providers: Silicon Data feeds CME, and Ornn feeds ICE and Kalshi. Tokenelo's fastest route to "trading AI compute as a commodity" is therefore to make the UCU index credible enough to license. EloCoin's job is to bootstrap the verified data supply that index needs. Speculative trading of ELO itself will not deliver that.

The plan also hinges on one assumption that has not been tested: that model providers will let their usage data drive token rewards. That question should be answered in week one, because a "no" changes the whole design. Without a provider anchor, the only verifiable path is hardware-attested inference on open models. That would turn EloCoin into a supply-side compute network. Legally that is cleaner, because it fits the SEC's favored "work performed" framing. Commercially it is a crowded field where Render, Akash and io.net have already shown that real usage does not hold up a token's price.
